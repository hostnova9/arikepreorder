ArikePreorder v0.10.1 — Admin Communications

Added Admin Communications to the existing Admin Centre.
- Send to selected users, all buyers, approved sellers, or everyone.
- In-app, branded email, or both.
- ArikePreorder branded email template and security footer.
- Sent-message audit/history with recipient and delivery counts.
- Email is only marked accepted when the configured provider accepts it.
- In-app messages work without email configuration.

Optional email environment variables:
RESEND_API_KEY=...
ARIKE_FROM_EMAIL=ArikePreorder <notifications@your-verified-domain.com>

Never commit the real .env file.
