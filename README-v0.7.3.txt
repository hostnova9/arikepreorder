ArikePreorder v0.7.3 - Cart Drawer + Smart Notifications

Changes:
- Top-right cart opens a right-side shopping drawer.
- Drawer shows cart items, subtotal, View Full Cart and Checkout.
- Checkout links to the payment section on the full cart page.
- Clicking outside the cart closes it; Escape also closes it.
- Notification panel closes when clicking anywhere outside it.
- Opening notifications marks current notifications as read.
- Notifications created in the last 12 hours rotate in the marketplace banner.
- After 12 hours they stop appearing in the banner but remain in notification history.
- VIP Shopper and VIP Seller moved from dashboard content into the sidebar.
- Existing marketplace, Help Pay, onboarding, KYC, seller and anonymous comments are preserved.

Keep your existing backend/.env when replacing the project.
