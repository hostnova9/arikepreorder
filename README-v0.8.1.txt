ArikePreorder v0.8.1 — Account + Seller Centre Restructure

Changes:
- Personal Account reorganized into controlled expandable Manage/Edit sections.
- Personal information, delivery address, security/PIN, KYC, notifications and privacy are no longer all exposed at once.
- Existing transaction PIN is no longer presented as a normal overwrite form.
- Seller Centre is visually and structurally separate from shopper Account.
- Users without a business see the Become/Register Seller application.
- Existing sellers get a dedicated Seller Centre navigation: Overview, Products, Orders, Money & Withdrawals, Business Profile.
- Seller products/orders/earnings remain connected to the v0.8 backend.
- Business profile and status are surfaced inside Seller Centre.
- Existing marketplace, cart drawer, smart notifications, VIP sidebar and onboarding are preserved.

Keep your existing backend/.env when replacing your project.
