ArikePreorder v0.9.4 — Seller Earnings + Business Registration + Banner Cleanup

- Clarifies personal Wallet vs Seller Earnings.
- Seller Earnings has Held and Available balances.
- Adds PIN-protected Available Earnings -> Wallet internal transfer.
- Held Earnings cannot be transferred.
- Internal transfers create linked seller-wallet and wallet transaction ledger records.
- Business application now collects Nigerian state, city/LGA, real operating address and location confirmation.
- CAC registration is optional; CAC fields appear only when seller selects Yes.
- Banner notifications are deduplicated and personal banner entries remain limited to the 12-hour window.
- Carousel surfaces use calmer ArikePreorder plum/earth/mint tones with less bright white.
