STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP & OWNERSHIP PROPOSAL

Build the marketplace. Grow the opportunity.

ArikePreorder is building a protected social-commerce marketplace around verified sellers, product discovery, structured orders, Help Pay My Cart, seller operations and platform-led support.

The proposal

We want to discuss a long-term strategic partnership in ArikePreorder, including an agreed ownership/shareholding interest subject to responsibilities, milestones and formal legal documentation. A ₦0 initial cash-entry structure may be discussed where strategic contribution, promotion, relationships and business development form part of the value contributed.

Where ArikePreorder is today

The platform is already under active development with buyer accounts, seller onboarding and verification, marketplace feeds, cart and checkout, Help Pay, wallet/payment infrastructure, Seller Centre, Admin Centre, Trending controls, notifications and protected product enquiries. The next stage is production testing, payment/compliance completion, partnerships, policies, infrastructure and launch growth.

What the partnership can contribute

We are looking beyond a one-time endorsement. The relationship can contribute audience growth, strategic introductions, launch promotion, credibility, market access, business development and long-term direction. Exact ownership, voting rights, vesting, responsibilities, dividend rights and exit terms would only become effective through signed corporate agreements.

OptiSave launch support

OptiSave is a separate savings-focused project and is not the investment or ownership being offered on this page. As part of the broader strategic relationship, we would like the ArikePreorder partner to support OptiSave's public introduction and promotion when it is ready to launch, helping communicate its savings benefits to the community. OptiSave ownership remains separate unless independently negotiated later.

Why it can matter to the community

ArikePreorder aims to make local online commerce more organized and accountable while giving verified businesses a structured storefront. OptiSave aims to encourage stronger saving discipline and goal-based financial habits. Together, the relationship can help us introduce useful products to a wider community while building sustainable value for both parties.
